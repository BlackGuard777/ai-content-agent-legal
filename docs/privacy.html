Privacy Policy

AI Content Agent ("the app") · Last updated: 7 November 2026

AI Content Agent is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner turn long videos into short clips and keep track of which clips were published. It is not a hosted service and has no user accounts of its own.

What the app accesses

  • The owner's own videos and files, stored locally on the owner's computer.
  • TikTok (if the owner connects an account): basic profile information and the list of the owner's own videos (title, description, duration, link), obtained through the TikTok API with the owner's permission. Used only to detect which clips have been published.
  • YouTube (if the owner connects a channel): the owner's own video list and their publishing status, and, only when the owner chooses, uploading a video as private. Used only to detect which clips have been published and to show basic statistics.

How data is used and stored

All data, including access tokens, is stored locally on the owner's computer. Tokens are encrypted by the operating system where supported. The app does not run a server that collects data, does not sell data, and does not share it for advertising.

Third-party services

To generate titles, descriptions and highlight suggestions, text transcribed from the owner's own videos may be sent to the Google Gemini API. The app also communicates with the TikTok and YouTube APIs as described above. The use of YouTube data is subject to the Google Privacy Policy, and the app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. The use of TikTok data is subject to TikTok's terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can disconnect TikTok or YouTube at any time, remove the app's access from the account's security settings on each platform, and delete all local data by deleting the app's folder.

Children

The app is not directed at children.
